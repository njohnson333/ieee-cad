What is CAD?

CAD stands for computer-aided design. It lets you create precise digital sketches and 3D models to plan, test, and make real parts.

01 / DESIGN

CAD Fundamentals

Sketches, constraints, dimensions, extrusions, assemblies, and the core workflow behind parametric modeling.

02 / PROTOTYPE

3D Printing

Prepare models for fabrication and learn how design decisions affect printability, strength, fit, and function.

03 / BUILD

Engineering Projects

Apply your skills through hands-on projects that challenge you to solve real design problems with your team.

Projects

Your portfolio should show more than software skills. These projects are designed to demonstrate engineering thinking, iteration, and fabrication.

PROJECT / A

Functional Part

Design a useful component with accurate dimensions, tolerances, and a clear purpose.

PROJECT / B

Mechanical Assembly

Create multiple interacting components and practice fit, movement, and assembly design.

PROJECT / C

Original HAC Project

Develop your own concept, iterate on it, fabricate it, and present the final result.

Brand new to CAD? Try a personalized keychain first: sketch a shape, set dimensions, extrude it, round the edges, and cut a hole for the ring.
END OF SEMESTER

HAC Showcase

The showcase is where members present what they designed and built during the semester. Participants should attend at least 70% of CAD classes to be eligible to showcase their project.
